# Tracer study

Studied on 2026-09-12 before changing the renderer. All images remain excluded from publication.

Night: [Commando Zed Time demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6wnE3USXSQE), 01:30 to 02:10.
Daylight: [Hell on Earth Outpost, Commando](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s8uYV8AMUjo), 03:00 to 03:40.
The Outpost footage uses overcast snow lighting, without direct sunlight.
yt-dlp 2026.08.19 downloaded both video-only sections at 480p or lower.
ffmpeg extracted 640 x 360 frames. The March downloader failed with HTTP 403.
Night frames sample 01:48 at 15 fps. Daylight frames sample 03:11 at 15 fps.
Numbered suffixes identify extracted frames, starting at 01. Clips stay in ignored local scratch storage.

1. Night fire has a white centre against dark masonry; separate white core energy from the coloured halo.
2. Orange muzzle fringes remain narrow; retain saturated orange around the rifle core without a broad light cloud.
3. End-on rifle shots collapse near the sight line; add a screen-length floor alongside the world-space trail.
4. Distant shot detail is poorly resolved at 640 pixels; compression prevents exact core-width measurements.
5. The requested exaggeration needs more width than these samples; use approximately three times the current player widths.
6. Short end-on streaks occupy a small screen fraction; use 112 pixels for the M4 floor at 1080p.
7. Snow and sky reduce white-effect contrast; preserve the coloured halo while increasing the independent white core.
8. Adjacent frames show brief shot light and longer smoke; keep the tracer transient and separate from smoke.
9. These clips do not resolve a reliable spatial fade curve; the rear-third fade follows the owner's requirement.
10. The footage cannot establish metres or normal-time speed; choose 8 m rifle and 14 m sniper trails at 150 m/s.

These are visual adaptations. They are not measured KF2 engine constants or a claim of an exact reproduction.
