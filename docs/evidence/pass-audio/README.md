# Audio pass

Branch `pass/audio`. Changes are limited to `lib/audio`, `lib/ui/sfx.js`, `assets/audio`, and this evidence directory. No dependencies added, no publish. All browser work used headless Chromium with the Metal backend. Output was muted to avoid disturbing the owner.

Implemented: 101 Ogg Opus files totaling 2,707,571 bytes (2.71 MB); real recorded pistol, AR-15 and shotgun close/mid-distance layers; reloads and surface foley; layered motors, pneumatics, impacts and explosions; UI and alarm samples; music/effects/UI/voice buses; 10 Hz sight-blocker low-pass probes; distance attenuation; one shared short room impulse with tunnel/building sends; gunfire and voice ducking; original 32-second ambient/combat loops; three score stingers; 12 processed offline Zarvox lines. The existing cue IDs remain valid. The missing local death cue is in `lib/ui/sfx.js`. Co-op shots, reloads, impacts and dry fire use the appropriate player identity.

Sources: [Free Firearm Sound Library](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library), [Kenney Impact](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), [Kenney Sci-fi](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds), [Kenney RPG](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio), [Kenney Interface](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), [recorded reloads](https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds), [shotgun shells](https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects), [alarm](https://opengameart.org/content/short-alarm). Every encoded file has an entry in `assets/audio/LICENSES.md`. Apple speech output is identified separately from CC0 recordings.

Validation: `npm test` passed 83/83. `npx kite3d check` passed Playable, Editable and Persisted. Editable retains the engine warning `CAMERA_CONTAINMENT_UNVERIFIED`. `node docs/evidence/pass-audio/verify.mjs` passed 15 browser assertions: all 101 decodes, non-silent PCM, all cue IDs, occlusion, buses, ducking, voice limits, local/remote ownership, unchanged gameplay snapshot, death cue, and complete Stop cleanup. The single synthesized playback in the results is the explicitly exercised heartbeat from the all-cue test. No sample failed to load. `lifecycle.mjs` additionally checks restart, cancellation during loading, and missing-file fallback. Neither browser test requires visible windows.

Performance: 36.56 ms mean, 33.30 ms median, 66.60 ms p95, 27.35 FPS. 240 frames at a true 1920 by 1080 drawing buffer, render scale 1, Apple M3 Max Metal. Exactly 24 enemies (eight per type) remained alive throughout. Default enemy brains and all view effects ran. The fixture gave the player high HP to keep the encounter alive, injected 20 nonlethal hit events and four explosion events, and triggered rifle audio. Observed 20 spark bursts, 144 plasma shots, 588 minigun shots, and 1084 sampled audio plays. Audio sync averaged 0.92 ms, p95 2.40 ms; this measures JS binding work, not the separate audio rendering thread. No other user processes were stopped. **The 60 FPS requirement is not met.**

Screenshots (1920 by 1080): `24-enemies.png` shows the measured encounter; `tunnel.png` shows the service-tunnel room position. Simulation is frozen only after timing, for screenshots and controlled local-death verification. Screenshots were visually inspected. The unchanged visuals remain visibly below KF2.

Remaining gaps: most paired gun variants are pitch/EQ variations of a single close take plus a separate mid-distance take. Minigun fire is assembled from rifle recordings. Metal footsteps and hydraulic movement are composites, and sci-fi source material is designed audio rather than recorded industrial machinery. Room acoustics use one short impulse with different send levels, without diffraction or separate measured room responses. The music is a small original industrial score, and the voice is processed TTS. Headless checks validate signals and behavior, not a headphone listening/mix approval. No subjective KF2-quality claim is made. Apple voice output redistribution is not cleared by the CC0 source licenses.

Still synthesized: the low-health heartbeat and room impulse at runtime; original music/stingers and speech generated offline into Ogg; procedural legacy recipes only when a sample fails to load or decode. Retaining these fallback recipes keeps old hooks functional during asset failures. Sampled lasers, motors and alarm retain the electronic nature of their CC0 source designs.
