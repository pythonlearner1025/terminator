Each retained sheet is 1920 x 1080. Each sheet includes real references and an actual Bunker 7 capture.
The external folder is `/Users/minjunes/games/terminator-evidence/docs/evidence/barrel/`.
All sheets were opened before the decisions below. Pixel readings are approximate.

Before round one, the bounds assertion rejected an invalid Boolean result.
Blender's Manifold solver fixed the closed shell cut. The temporary failed sheet was replaced.

## Round 1

Opened `round-1.png`, including its three views, two crops, references, and runtime panel.

1. Blue paint covers approximately 55 percent of the front; the burn photo has less than 10 percent.
2. Rust transitions span 10 to 30 pixels; the burn photo has much finer 1 to 6 pixel marks.
3. The rim width is 0.017 m, or 4.3 percent of radius; the open-head photo suggests 2 to 3 percent.
4. The ember floor shows about 12 regular radial lines; the photo contains irregular wood and coals.
5. The visible front dent is less than 3 pixels; the reference mouth silhouette has approximately 6 pixels of asymmetry.

Next: darken surviving paint, sharpen rust breakup, thin the lip, unwrap a square coal pocket, and bring a dent forward.
The runtime fire uses large square particles. This existing effect remains outside the prop scope.

## Round 2

Opened `round-2.png`, the full three-quarter render, and the baked albedo atlas.

1. Rust islands now span approximately 5 to 18 pixels, but resemble evenly distributed camouflage.
2. A dent crosses the coarse inner wall and exposes a 40 x 65 pixel false opening.
3. The real impact holes measure 6 to 10 pixels; retain those two openings only.
4. Coal emission still forms approximately four long lines, instead of small separated bright pockets.
5. The rim crop has less than 5 percent fine bright abrasion; the real lip has a longer broken highlight.

Next: align inner rings with the shell, use mostly oxidized charcoal steel, retain finer photographic normals, and localize embers.

## Round 3

Opened `round-3.png`. The false opening is gone, with two intended holes still visible.

1. The shell now has zero false openings; both intended holes remain approximately 6 pixels wide in the sheet.
2. The front rim still follows a nearly perfect ellipse, with less than 2 pixels of local warp.
3. Hole edges have no clear 1 to 2 pixel abrasion ring, unlike the damaged fire photograph.
4. The coal pocket still reads as about ten bright lines on black, rather than glowing solid fragments.
5. The 600 x 720 source renders undersample the 1024 maps in the 4x crops; small pores disappear after denoising.

Next: warp one rim segment, bake impact-edge wear, add low coal glow, and render final crops at twice the source resolution.

## Round 4

Opened `round-4.png`, the full three-quarter view, and `four-barrels-fire.png`.
The final studio renders use 1200 x 1440 source pixels; retained sheets use 1920 x 1080.

1. The lip now has a 7.5 mm local dent, or 1.9 percent of radius, near the reference asymmetry.
2. Both holes have a visible one-pixel metal edge in the sheet; the photo has more irregular torn edges.
3. The floor now has small curved ember patches, replacing the previous long straight radial lines.
4. Hoop spacing remains 0.322 of height, inside the 0.29 to 0.35 measured reference range.
5. The 4x crop still looks softer than the photo. The 1024 atlas and denoising limit close inspection.

Four rounds are complete. The shape and dark material read as a used open steel drum at gameplay scale.
This does not establish photo parity or KF2 shipping quality. No verified KF2 burn-barrel close-up was obtained.
The existing square fire particles and bright unshadowed mouth light remain the largest in-scene differences.
All four original placements remain unchanged. Each runtime capture has four fire systems and one merged barrel batch.
