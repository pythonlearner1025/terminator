## Trace provenance and physical scale

`profiles.json` stores image coordinates, traced component polygons, and converted metre coordinates.
The left outline comes from arsenal-03.jpg. The top outline comes from arsenal-01.jpg, upper photograph only.
The 203 mm barrel spans the muzzle to cylinder front. It does not mean 203 mm beyond the frame.
Left scale: 0.000232798 m/px. Top scale: 0.000248775 m/px.
Frame, opening, grip, lever, hammer, trigger, bow, barrel, and sight have separately traced polygons.
The cylinder uses its photographed side radius profile and the independently measured top diameter.
The top photograph has perspective and grip occlusion. Its silhouette is a view-dependent target.
The six additional images come from [College Hill Arsenal](https://collegehillarsenal.com/Fine-Remington-New-Model-Army-Revolver).
They are study references only. They are not CC0 textures and never enter the game assets.

The later support-palm trace is stored under `hand_left` in profiles.json.
Its 14 measured outer points use KF2 frame 04 and the original 52-degree projection for reconstruction.
The final exported presentation uses a 44-degree field of view, as recorded in the round log.
