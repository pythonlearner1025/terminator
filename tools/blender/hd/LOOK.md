1. Keep the source length and grip pivot fixed during every comparison.
2. Preserve the broad receiver panels. Spend geometry on their rims.
3. Give the barrel and magazine cap continuous curved highlights.
4. Use dark bluing with narrow bright reflections, as shown in the revolver photographs.
5. Keep wood grain longitudinal, with softer roughness changes than steel wear.
6. Place cavity dirt inside grooves, not uniformly across the surface.
7. Keep stock seams narrow. The original shotgun already has a bead sight.
8. Add cylinder flute relief without enlarging the original cylinder envelope.
9. Bake small checkering from distinct high-poly detail into the normal map.
10. Report gate failures explicitly. Triangle counts alone do not establish quality.
